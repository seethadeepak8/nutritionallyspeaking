A little about me

Clinical knowledge. A human approach.

I’m Seethalakshmi Deepak (RD, MS, CDE), a nutrition professional with 15+ years of experience across clinical nutrition, diabetes care, specialised nutrition and digital health.

My work has included supporting people with complex health needs, developing nutrition programmes, mentoring and training multidisciplinary teams, and contributing to quality and patient education. Across hospital and digital-health settings, one thing has remained constant: nutrition works best when the science is translated into guidance people can understand and sustain.

Alongside consultations, I enjoy teaching and communicating nutrition science—connecting research and clinical practice with the questions people ask about food, health and wellbeing.
